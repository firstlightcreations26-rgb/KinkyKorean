I'm an Open Book… Until It Actually Matters

I'm an open book and apparently lack some serious fucking skills when it comes to boundaries.

I swear I wasn't listening as a child when they were explaining the whole “some things are better left unsaid” concept. 😂

As an adult, I SUCK at keeping things to myself. I tell on myself constantly.

I'm terrible at lying. I've always thought honesty is the best policy, but I'm slowly realizing that sometimes the best policy is probably just… Shut the fuck up, KK.

Do I learn my lesson after I say too much and immediately kick myself afterward? Absolutely not. Clearly.

I mean, LOOK AT THIS WEBSITE. I literally created an entire diary on the internet filled with my dirty little secrets.

Could this eventually bite me in the ass? Maybe? Do I care at the moment? Not really. 😂

Because the truth is, I'm not ashamed of my sexual past. I had a great time. I love sex. There's no shame in my game.

I think that's one thing that makes me different from some women. I'll own my shit. I'm not interested in pretending I've always been some innocent little angel just because that version sounds prettier.

Please. Some of you little hoe bags know exactly what I'm talking about. 😂 I'm just the idiot writing mine down for strangers to read.

I'm more like… if I'm single, the vibe is right, the person is right, and we're having a good time? Well. KK likes a good time. 💋

But I'm not single. I'm in a relationship that I genuinely love being in. I respect him. I adore him.

And this is where my whole “I'm an open book” thing suddenly makes absolutely no fucking sense. Because I'll tell YOU guys all this shit… but sometimes I struggle to tell him what's actually going on inside my head.

I will completely own the fact that I can be an awful communicator in my relationship. Sometimes I think he should magically know what I'm thinking. Apparently men still haven't developed telepathy. Rude.

He knows I'm an emotional roller coaster, and somehow he handles me incredibly well. I want to be completely honest and vulnerable with him, but there's something inside me that keeps slamming on the brakes.

I want to feel close to him on every level. I can feel when there's distance between us. It bothers the shit out of me. And yet… sometimes I still don't do anything about it.

Sometimes I wish I had the balls to simply trust that I could tell him what I'm feeling without being judged, criticized, rejected, or looked at differently.

He's given me plenty of reasons to trust his love. He's shown it to me over and over again. But there's still this tiny stubborn part of me whispering: Don't trust it too much. Don't get too comfortable. Don't give somebody enough of you that they could really hurt you.

Growing up, I learned not to completely trust people because eventually they disappoint you, leave you, hurt you, or prove that their words didn't mean what you thought they meant.

Except I'm not a kid anymore. And that protection that probably made sense once? Sometimes it fucks with the life I actually want now.

Because here I am… comfortable enough to put my sexual history, ridiculous stories, embarrassing moments, and dirty little secrets into an internet diary… but terrified to sit across from the man I love and say: “Here's what's actually going on inside me.”

Maybe that's the boundary I got completely backwards.

Maybe being an open book isn't about telling everybody everything. Maybe it's learning who deserves to read the pages that actually matter.

And I think he does. Now I just have to figure out how to let him read them.

— KK 💋
